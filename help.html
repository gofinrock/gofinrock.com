How do I connect my bank account?
Paid plans: tap Settings → Accounts → Add account. Read-only access via Open Banking — we never see your bank login. Takes 60 seconds; transactions appear within 5 minutes. Free plan: upload CSV or PDF statements instead.
Is Finrock free?
The Personal tier is free forever (Life Tracker, bills, MOT reminders, document vault, bill-savings alerts). First-year intro: Pro (sole trader) £14.99/mo, Business (Ltd) £24.99/mo, Business+ (Ltd with payroll) £34.99/mo. Standard from year 2: £19.99 / £29.99 / £44.99. Every paid plan includes a UK accountant who files for you. 14-day free trial. See Pricing.
How do I import historical transactions?
After connecting your bank, Finrock automatically pulls 12 months of history. You can also upload a CSV via Settings → Import for older data.
Can I switch banks?
Yes — connect as many as you like (1 on Personal tier, unlimited on Pro & Business). Disconnect anytime in Settings.
How does the accountant filing work?
When you're ready to file, Finrock locks the books and routes them to a qualified accountant from our UK panel. They review (usually 24–48h), email you for any queries, and submit to HMRC. Included on Pro & Business.

Still stuck?

Pro and Business users get 1-hour priority chat. Personal users get email replies within 24h.
