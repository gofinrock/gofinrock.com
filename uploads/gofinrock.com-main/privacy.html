Privacy policy

Effective: 22 May 2026 · Last updated: 22 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Finrock Ltd ("Finrock", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares personal data when you use our website (gofinrock.com) and our mobile and desktop apps (the "Service").

1. Who we are

Finrock Ltd is a company registered in England & Wales. Our registered office is in London. For any privacy queries, contact privacy@gofinrock.com.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email, phone, business name, VAT number, NI number
  • Documents you upload to your Vault (passport, MOT, will, etc.)
  • Receipts, invoices, payslips and other financial documents
  • Communications with our support team

2.2 Information we receive automatically

  • Transaction data from connected bank accounts (read-only, via Open Banking) — paid plans only
  • Device information: IP address, OS, app version, crash logs
  • Usage analytics: pages visited, features used (no third-party trackers)

3. How we use your data

To provide the Service: AI categorisation of transactions, document expiry tracking, bill comparison, tax calculation, payment processing, and routing returns to qualified accountants on our panel.

4. Who we share it with

  • HMRC, when you submit a tax return through us
  • Open Banking providers (TrueLayer) — bank connection only
  • Stripe and GoCardless — payment processing
  • Accountants on our UK panel — only when you initiate a filing
  • Cloud infrastructure (AWS, UK region) — never outside the UK without your consent

We never sell your data. We never share it for marketing purposes.

5. How long we keep it

Financial records: 7 years from the end of the tax year they relate to (UK statutory requirement). Vault documents: until you delete them. Account data: deleted within 30 days of account closure.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can request access to your data, correct it, delete it, port it to another provider, or restrict our processing of it. Email privacy@gofinrock.com — we respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard methods. Bank access is read-only. Two-factor authentication is required on every account. See our Security page for details.

8. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies only (session, CSRF, theme preference). No advertising trackers. No third-party analytics. See our Cookies page.

9. Changes

We'll notify you of material changes by email at least 30 days in advance.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@gofinrock.com. Data Protection Officer: dpo@gofinrock.com. You can also complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.
