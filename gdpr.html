GDPR statement

Effective: 22 May 2026

Finrock complies with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This page summarises how we apply those rules.

Your rights under UK GDPR

  • Right of access — request a copy of all data we hold about you (CSV export, free of charge, within 30 days)
  • Right to rectification — correct any inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — close your account; we delete data within 30 days (except statutorily required financial records)
  • Right to data portability — export everything in a machine-readable format (CSV, JSON, OFX)
  • Right to restrict processing — pause our use of your data without deleting it
  • Right to object — object to specific processing purposes (e.g. opt out of analytics)
  • Right to not be subject to automated decision-making — our AI is decision-support; you confirm every transaction
