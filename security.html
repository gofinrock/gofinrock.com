How we keep your money, identity and documents safe.

🔒

AES-256 encryption

Every vault document encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Keys rotated quarterly. Decryption only happens server-side at moment of access.

🏦

Open Banking certified

Bank connections via FCA-regulated TrueLayer. Read-only. We never see your bank login. Revoke access anytime from your bank's app.

🇬🇧

UK-hosted

All data hosted in UK datacentres (AWS eu-west-2). No data leaves the UK without your explicit consent. GDPR by default.

🛡

2-factor authentication

Required for every account. TOTP (Authy, Google Authenticator) or SMS fallback. Hardware key support on Pro & Business.

⚠

SOC 2 Type II (in progress)

Type I completed Q1 2026. Type II audit window currently open, results expected Q4 2026. Pen-tested twice a year by external firm.

✉

Disclosure programme

Found a vulnerability? security@gofinrock.com. We respond within 24h, bounties from £250–£10,000 depending on severity.
