Common questions

Anything to clear up?

How does "filed by accountant" work?
Finrock prepares MTD-compliant VAT, Self Assessment and corporation tax returns. A qualified accountant from our UK panel reviews and files them with HMRC on your behalf. You see every step. No bookkeeper bills. Included with Pro & Business plans.
Is one app really for personal AND business?
Yes. One login, two modes. Switch with one tap. Your personal life and your business books live side by side — never mixed up, never duplicated.
How do you save me money on bills?
We scan your statements, compare bills against UK averages, and surface cheaper plans. We link you directly to the provider — you switch with them, we never act as a broker. Designed by accountants who got tired of clients overpaying.
Is my data safe?
Read-only access to your bank data. We never see your login. UK-hosted. You can delete your account and all data with one tap from Settings.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. One tap in Settings. Data exports as CSV. No long contracts.
