Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Effective: 22 May 2026

This DPA forms part of the Finrock Terms of Service and applies when you, as a business user (Controller), use Finrock (Processor) to process personal data of third parties (e.g. your customers, suppliers, employees).

1. Roles

You are the Data Controller. Finrock is the Data Processor.

2. Scope of processing

We process personal data only on your documented instructions, as needed to provide the Service (storage, AI categorisation, payment processing, tax filing).

3. Sub-processors

We use these sub-processors, all UK-based or with Standard Contractual Clauses in place:

AWS (UK datacentres) · TrueLayer (Open Banking) · Stripe (payments) · GoCardless (Direct Debit) · Resend (email) · Twilio (SMS).

We notify you 30 days before adding new sub-processors. You can object by contacting dpo@gofinrock.com.

4. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard methods. Bank access is read-only. Two-factor authentication is required on every account. See our Security page.

5. Breach notification

We notify you within 24 hours of becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting your data.

6. Audit rights

On-site audits available for Business and Enterprise customers with 30 days notice.

7. Term & return of data

This DPA continues for the duration of your Finrock subscription. On termination we return or delete personal data within 30 days at your choice.

8. Contact

Data Protection Officer: dpo@gofinrock.com
