Cookies policy

Effective: 22 May 2026

Finrock uses the smallest set of cookies possible. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party trackers, or any cross-site tracking technology.

Cookies we use

CookiePurposeDuration
fr_sessionMaintains your signed-in session30 days

Analytics

We use self-hosted Plausible Analytics to understand how the website is used. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't collect personal data, and doesn't share anything with third parties. You can see what we see at plausible.gofinrock.com — fully public.

Managing cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser. The strictly necessary ones (fr_session, fr_csrf) are required for the app to work; without them you can browse the marketing site but can't sign in.
