FAQ

Common questions from side-hustlers.

I'm under £30k from side income. Do I still need MTD?
Not yet — but you still need to file Self Assessment if your side income is over £1,000 (the trading allowance). The Personal tier handles tracking; upgrade to Pro when you want a Finrock accountant to file your SA.
Can I deduct my Netflix subscription from tax?
No — and we won't let you. AI tags Netflix as personal automatically. Things that are tax-deductible (a portion of your phone bill, mileage to client meetings, home-office utilities pro-rated) get booked separately to your side-hustle.
I'm a landlord with one rental flat. Is that "self-employment"?
No — it's "property income", a separate category for tax. But it counts the same for MTD purposes (over £50k from April 2026, £30k from April 2027). We handle property income the same way: AI categorises, accountant files.
What if I have a 9-to-5 AND a side hustle AND a rental?
Then you've got the full triple. Your PAYE income is automatic (HMRC has it). The side hustle and rental need declaring on Self Assessment — and from £30k combined, MTD-compatible software is mandatory. That's exactly what we do.
I missed a few years of Self Assessment. Can you help?
Yes — a Finrock accountant can file back returns (a "voluntary disclosure"). We charge a one-off fee on top of subscription for back-years. Less stress than HMRC finding it themselves.
