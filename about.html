About Finrock

Built by people who hated
doing the books.

Finrock is the Life OS we wished existed when we started our last company.

The story.

Our founder built a B2B SaaS for 6 years. He never once enjoyed the bookkeeping. The receipts piled up. The VAT quarter loomed. The Self Assessment took a weekend.

And on the personal side: the passport was 3 weeks expired before he noticed. The MOT lapsed. The energy provider auto-renewed at 28% over market. The Spotify Family plan kept four ex-roommates riding free.

One app watched your spending. Another took a percentage of your income. A third needed a training course. Nobody connected the dots. So we built the dots-connector. AI watches, calmly. You live your life.

The principles.

1 · Calm, not chatty

No emoji. No "great job 🎉". Adult tone, accountant-grade. The 55-year-old test: would a chartered accountant onboarding a client wince?

2 · We already know

Don't ask the user "Want to compare bills?" Tell them: "We can save you £312/yr — tap to switch." The work is already done; the user confirms.

3 · One pipeline, surfaces everywhere

Upload a passport → OCR extracts expiry → Life Dashboard tile shows it → Calendar event auto-created → 30d before, Needs Attention surfaces it. Same pipeline runs for bills, MOT, mortgage, HMRC dates.

4 · Accountants are partners

We're not replacing accountants — we're making clients arrive with finished books. Less re-keying, faster turnaround, better margins for the practice.
