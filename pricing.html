Pricing questions

Common ones, answered.

Why is Personal free?
Because most people pay £30/mo across Emma, Snoop, AnyDo, etc. We replace all of them. Free tier earns us trust; you'll upgrade when you go freelance or open a Ltd company.
Do you take a % of my revenue?
No. Flat price. Hnry takes 1% which on £80k revenue is £800/yr — we're £107.88/yr. Bigger you grow, more sense we make.
What's the bill-switching savings guarantee?
If you don't save more in bills than you pay us in your first year on Pro or Business, we refund the subscription. Average user saves £492/yr — most users break even in month one.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. One click in Settings. Your data stays accessible for 90 days in case you change your mind. You can also export everything as CSV/QBO at any time.
VAT-registered? Foreign currencies?
Pro and Business handle VAT MTD (standard, flat-rate, cash-accounting), multi-currency, and EU VAT for B2B services. Reverse-charge supported. CIS for construction.
