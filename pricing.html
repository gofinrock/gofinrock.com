Pricing questions

Common ones, answered.

Why is Personal free?
Because juggling separate apps for net worth, bills, MOT, passport and pension is exhausting. We do it all in one. Free tier earns us trust; you'll upgrade when you go freelance or open a Ltd.
Do you take a % of my revenue?
No. Flat monthly price, full stop. £14.99 in your first year (£19.99 standard) regardless of whether you earn £20k or £200k. The bigger you grow, the more sense we make.
What's the bill-savings guarantee?
If our cheaper-plan alerts don't save you more than your subscription cost in year one, we refund the subscription. We surface the cheaper plan; you switch directly with the provider.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. One click in Settings. Your data stays accessible for 90 days in case you change your mind. You can also export everything as CSV/QBO at any time.
VAT-registered? Foreign currencies?
Pro and Business handle VAT MTD (standard, flat-rate, cash-accounting), multi-currency, and EU VAT for B2B services. Reverse-charge supported. CIS for construction.
